## What it is
The debugging discipline for any bug, test failure, crash or unexpected behaviour (code, scripts, an app on the phone).

## What it does
Find the root cause before proposing a fix; change one thing at a time; stop after three failed fixes and rethink.

## How it does it
A `SKILL.md` in `~/.claude/skills/systematic-debugging`; Claude loads it when a request matches its description.

## Where it sits in the pipeline
Part of the development process in Claude Code, used in every project.

## Who receives its output
Claude, while fixing; the fix then goes through [[skill-verification-before-completion]].
