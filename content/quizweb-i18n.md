## What it is
Language handling of the web twin, ported from the app: which language to show, UI text lookup, calendar names and translated explanation text.

## What it does
An explicit choice wins; otherwise the first browser language it offers, else English. Questions and answer options stay German.

## How it does it
`strings.js` is generated by [[gen-web-strings]]; `i18n/*.json` are copies of the app's content translations, compared by [[check-web-sync]].

## Where it sits in the pipeline
Used by [[quizweb-app]].

## Who receives its output
[[quizweb-app]].
