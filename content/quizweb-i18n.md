## What it is
i18n ("internationalisation": i, 18 letters, n) is the handling of several languages. `i18n.js` and `strings.js` give [[quizweb]] its language handling, ported from the app: which language to show, looking up interface text, calendar names and translated explanation text.

## What it does
An explicit choice by the user wins; otherwise the first browser language it offers is used, otherwise English. The questions and answer options stay German.

## How it does it
`strings.js` is generated by [[gen-web-strings]]; the files `i18n/*.json` are copies of the app's content translations, compared by [[check-web-sync]].

## Where it sits in the pipeline
Used by [[quizweb-app]].

## Who receives its output
[[quizweb-app]].
