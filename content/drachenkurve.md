## What it is
A Processing sketch that draws the Heighway dragon curve, exported as a standalone app.

## What it does
Rendered the launcher icon of [[quizapp]] (version 2.1): a dragon curve running from pastel pink to red on a dark background.

## How it does it
Processing (`drachenkurve.pde`) with an exported `build/`.

## Where it sits in the pipeline
Its one output went into [[quizapp-app]]'s icon resources.

## Who receives its output
[[quizapp-app]] (the icon).
