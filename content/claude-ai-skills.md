## What it is
The skills available to the [[claude-app]] (shown to Claude Code as `anthropic-skills:*`). Most are the app's own versions of the local [[skills]]; some exist only there.

## What it does
App-only ones include: docs, docx, pdf, pptx, xlsx and google-workspace (documents and Google files), import-memory, morning (a morning brief), setup-writing-style, skill-creator, and deutsch-gespraech-memory ([[deutsch-gespraech]]). gui-code-protocol is still listed there but belongs to the retired pipeline ([[retired-workflow-pipeline]]).

## How it does it
Managed in the Claude app; each side keeps its own version ("edited only by Claude Code; the app keeps its own version"). Claude Code cannot see their contents change, so this page carries a review date instead of sources.

## Where it sits in the pipeline
Off the laptop.

## Who receives its output
[[claude-app]].
