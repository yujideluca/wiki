## What it is
Diagnoses and fixes problems of Giovanni's laptop (Acer Nitro, Kubuntu 24.04, Plasma, Nvidia) and makes the fixes survive reboots. Written in Portuguese.

## What it does
Covers display, drivers, audio, network, desktop and boot problems, step by step, with commands that need sudo handed to Giovanni.

## How it does it
A `SKILL.md` in `~/.claude/skills/reparo-kubuntu`; Claude loads it when a request matches its description.

## Where it sits in the pipeline
Maintenance of the laptop itself.

## Who receives its output
