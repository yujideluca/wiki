## What it is
The built output: `index.html`, `assets/` (the compiled Elm app `app.js`, `boot.js`, `style.css`) and `data/` (graph and pages JSON).

## What it does
Everything the browser needs after login; nothing in it is ever sent before login.

## How it does it
Generated by [[wiki-build]], not committed (it is derived); served from disk by [[wiki-router]], so a publish shows up without restarting the server.

## Where it sits in the pipeline
Between [[wiki-build]] and [[wiki-router]].

## Who receives its output
[[wiki-router]].
