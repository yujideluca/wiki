## What it is
Giovanni's German training for the Goethe-Zertifikat B2 and then the Fachsprachprüfung (FSP, C1 medicine). Written in Portuguese.

## What it does
Corrects German sentences and texts, gives exercises, simulates exam parts (Schreiben, Sprechen, doctor–patient talk, Arztbrief, doctor–doctor talk), medical vocabulary PT–DE, grammar explanations and heuristics for verbs with prepositions, cases and declension.

## How it does it
A `SKILL.md` in `~/.claude/skills/alemao-goethe-fsp`; Claude loads it when a request matches its description.

## Where it sits in the pipeline
Giovanni's study and career work, outside the code projects.

## Who receives its output
Giovanni; errors found in corrected texts also fed the Fehler-finden mode of [[quizapp]].
